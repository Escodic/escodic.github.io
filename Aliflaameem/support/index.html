Common questions

Prayer times or the Qibla don't appear

The app needs your location to calculate them. Allow location access when asked, or go to More → Settings → Location and search for your city instead.

Prayer times differ from my masjid

On the Prayer Times screen, choose the calculation method used in your area (for example Karachi in Pakistan) and Hanafi or Shafi'i for Asr. Always follow your local masjid's jama'at times.

I don't get prayer reminders

Tap the bell next to each prayer on the Prayer Times screen and allow notifications. On Android 14 and later, also allow Alarms & reminders for on-time alerts. Open the app at least once a week so reminders stay scheduled.

How do I change the language?

Go to More → Settings → Language and choose English, اردو or العربية. The app restarts to switch between left-to-right and right-to-left.

The Qibla compass seems wrong

Hold the phone flat, away from metal and magnets, and move it in a figure-8 to calibrate the compass.

Can I see the manuscript pages?

Each manuscript links to the HMML Reading Room, where the full page images are available with a free vHMML account.
