Aliflaameem — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the Aliflaameem mobile app (for Android) and the Aliflaameem website handle your information. In short: there are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Your location and settings stay on your device, and we never sell or share personal information.

Who we are

Aliflaameem is an Islamic knowledge platform. The app and website were developed by Escodic. You can contact us at aliflaameem@gmail.com.

Your location

Prayer times, the Qibla direction and the Hijri calendar are calculated from your location. With your permission, the app reads your device's location, or you can choose a city in Settings instead.

  • The calculations happen on your device. Your location is not sent to us.
  • The app keeps your last location on your device so the home-screen widget and prayer reminders work. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • To show the name of your town, the app uses Android's built-in location-name service, and the website uses OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service; these receive your approximate coordinates for that lookup.
  • When you search for a city in Settings, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places.

Prayer reminders and notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders, the notifications are scheduled by your phone itself. No push-notification service is used and nothing is sent to us. The app asks for permission to show notifications and, for on-time reminders, to schedule exact alarms. You can turn reminders off in the app at any time.

When you contact us

The Contact Us form does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email app or WhatsApp with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. We receive only what you send — for example your email address, phone number (optional), message and any files you attach — and use it only to reply to you.

Content from other services

  • Videos are YouTube videos shown with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. When you play one, Google may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Manuscript images are loaded from the vHMML Reading Room of the Hill Museum & Manuscript Library (HMML). The Reading Room link opens HMML's website, which has its own privacy policy.
  • The verse of the day and hadith of the day are included in the app and the website; reading them sends nothing anywhere.

What is stored on your device

Your preferences — language, theme, prayer calculation settings, reminders, saved manuscripts and tasbeeh counts — are stored only on your device (or, on the website, in your browser). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (or your browser's site data) removes them.

Permissions the app uses

  • Location — to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • Notifications and exact alarms — for prayer reminders, if you turn them on.
  • Vibration — for the tasbeeh counter, if you leave vibration on.
  • Internet — for videos, manuscript images, city search and contact.

Children

The app and website are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no accounts or sign-up.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Questions are welcome at aliflaameem@gmail.com.
