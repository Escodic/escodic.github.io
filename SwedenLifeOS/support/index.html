Common questions

Is this an official government app?

No. Sweden Life OS is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Swedish government or any Swedish authority. Information in the app is general guidance, not official or legal advice. Always check the authority's own website, linked in the app, before you act.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password?. We'll send you a link to choose a new one. If you signed in with Google, your password is managed in your Google account.

How do I change my email or password?

Go to Settings → Manage account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Manage account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the details stored with it, and cannot be undone. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

I got a new phone and my checklist is gone

Your checklist, setup answers, documents and deadlines are stored only on your phone, not in your account. They move to a new phone only if your phone's own backup and restore brings app data across.

The app can't find my location

Check that location access is turned on for Sweden Life OS in your phone's settings. You can also choose your municipality by hand under Settings → Location.

I don't get deadline reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Sweden Life OS in your phone's settings. Reminders arrive at 09:00, before the deadline you added.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose English or Svenska.

How do I get my checklist suggestions again?

Go to Settings → Your answers and answer the setup questions again. Tasks you've marked as done are kept.

Emergencies

In an emergency in Sweden, always call 112. For healthcare advice, call 1177. This app is not an emergency service.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and your account stores only what it needs. Read the privacy policy.
