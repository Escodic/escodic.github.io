Common questions

There is no Swedish audio

SvenskaVägen uses your phone's built-in Swedish voice. If your phone doesn't have one, lessons show text instead and everything still works. To add a voice on Android, search Settings for Text-to-speech and install Swedish voice data. On iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Swedish. Then open Settings in the app and tap Check again.

Audio is installed but I hear nothing

Check that Play Swedish audio is on in the app's Settings and that your media volume is up. On iPhone, the silent switch also mutes speech.

The voice is too fast or too slow

In Settings → Pronunciation audio, choose Slow, Normal or Natural speed.

What level does the app teach?

SvenskaVägen starts from zero (Pre-A1) and builds step by step through A1, A2, B1 and B2 towards C1, with grammar, vocabulary, conversations and spaced review along the way.

I got a new phone and my progress is gone

There is no account, so progress is stored only on the phone where you study. It moves to a new phone only if your phone's own backup and restore brings app data across.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings → Your data → Reset all progress. This cannot be undone.

Can I change my daily goal?

Yes. In Settings → Daily goal, choose 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes a day.
