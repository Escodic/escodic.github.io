SvenskaVägen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the SvenskaVägen Swedish learning app handles your information. In short: there are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The app does not send your information anywhere — your progress and settings stay on your device, and we never sell or share personal information.

Who we are

SvenskaVägen is an app for learning Swedish, from first words to advanced conversation. It was developed by Escodic. You can contact us at tech@escodic.com.

Information we collect

None. SvenskaVägen has no accounts or sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests and never sends data to Escodic or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few things on your phone:

  • Your learning progress — completed lessons, review history, streak and XP.
  • Your settings — daily goal, appearance, audio speed, and audio and vibration choices.
  • The name you can optionally type in Settings.

We never receive any of this. It may be included in your phone's own backups if you use them (Google or iCloud backup). You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Your data → Reset all progress, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it stored.

Pronunciation audio

Swedish audio is spoken by your phone's own built-in text-to-speech voice, on your device. The app does not record audio and does not ask for microphone access.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibration — for gentle feedback on taps and answers, if you leave vibration on.
  • Internet — included by the app's framework. SvenskaVägen does not use it to send or receive any of your information.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. If you download it from Google Play or the App Store, Google's or Apple's own privacy policy applies to the store itself.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive only what you send — such as your email address and message — and use it only to reply to you.

Children

SvenskaVägen is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Questions are welcome at tech@escodic.com.
